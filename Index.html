Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, absolutely. Cancel anytime from your account settings with one click. No questions asked, no guilt trips. If you cancel, you'll have access until the end of your billing period, then it stops. Your data is yours — we'll help you export everything if you want.

Is there a free trial?

Pulse and Flux both offer 14-day free trials. No credit card required. After the trial ends, you'll be asked to confirm your subscription. We'll send you a reminder 2 days before so there are no surprises.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum). All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. Upgrade immediately and we'll pro-rate your next billing cycle. Downgrade at any time — it takes effect after your current billing period ends. We'll credit you fairly for any unused time.

Do you offer team or business plans?

Yes. The Omega tier is designed for teams and agencies. Contact our sales team for custom pricing based on your team size and needs. We offer volume discounts, SSO, custom integrations, and dedicated support.

How do creators earn money?

Creators on Nexus tier and above earn through:
• Listener tips (you keep 85%, Xavier takes 15%)
• Subscription revenue share (if subscribers, you get 50% of Pulse-Flux revenue)
• Sponsorships and brand partnerships
• Educational content (course sales)
• Monthly creator fund pool ($1M distributed to top performers)

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not happy, we'll refund 100% of your subscription fee, no questions asked. After 30 days, we offer pro-rata refunds if you cancel mid-month.
